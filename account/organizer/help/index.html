Organizer guide

Organizer checklist

A short list for the Downtown Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl. The crawl id is puy-coffee. The public page stays /puyallup/holiday-coffee-crawl. Run through this before launch Saturday, Oct 31.

Open the Organizer tab Store checklist Support

Step 1

Sign in

  1. Open /account and send yourself a magic link, the same way a guest does. There is no password.
  2. Tap Organizer. If it is missing, this email is not on the crawl team yet.
  3. Pick Downtown Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl in the crawl menu.

Step 2

Title and dates

  1. Under Title and dates, check the title, the start, and the end.
  2. Change anything that is off, then tap Save crawl.
  3. The end date needs to be on or after the start.

Step 3

Paid and hidden shops

  1. Under Stores, the counts show how many shops are paid and unpaid.
  2. Each shop says Paid or Unpaid.
  3. Show on the passport hides or shows that shop. Leave it checked when guests should see the stop.
  4. On a paid shop, tap Counter card next to Edit. You will see that shop's counter code and QR, even if the host has not opened the Store tab.
  5. Tap Print counter card or Download counter card. The download is the same folded tent. Print it on letter paper, landscape, and fold on the dashed line.

Step 4

Finish another shop's profile

  1. Under Stores, tap Edit on the shop. This works for a shop that is not yours.
  2. Fill the name, address, zip code, and hours. Add a cover photo, and a stamp image if they have one.
  3. Tap Save shop.
  4. The host can edit the same fields on their own Store tab. Share the store checklist when they want to do it themselves.

Step 5

Paper-card stamps

  1. A guest can check Play with a paper card when they sign up, then print at /card.
  2. On the Organizer tab, Print paper card opens a blank card. The same card is at /card?crawl=puy-coffee&blank=1.
  3. Under Players, search the guest by name, email, or phone. Use at least 2 characters.
  4. Check each shop they visited on paper. A digital stamp stays checked. You cannot clear it here.
  5. That check records the paper card. A phone stamp uses the shop QR or counter code. The phone does not ask for location.
  6. Completed paper cards must be turned in by end of day December 31, 2026 (Pacific).

We'll have giveaways for participants through the end of the year.

Step 6

Counter QR at Holiday Cafe and ANTHEM

Run this once at each counter before Saturday, October 31.

  1. On the Organizer tab, pick Downtown Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl.
  2. Find Holiday Cafe and ANTHEM. Each should say Paid, with Show on the passport checked.
  3. Tap Edit and finish anything blank: name, address, zip code, hours, and a photo. Tap Save shop.
  4. Under Stores, tap Counter card on Holiday Cafe, then on ANTHEM. Each card shows the counter code and the QR. This works before the host has claimed Store.
  5. Tap Print counter card and put the tent on that counter. Download counter card saves the same tent if you want to print it later.
  6. A host can still open the Store tab and tap Print counter sign. Both paths use the same code and QR. Do not post the code online.
  7. A guest signs in with an email magic link, then scans the QR or types the code and picks that shop.
  8. The phone does not ask for location. The stamp fills in from that code or QR. If the host turned on a post-stamp coupon, the guest sees it after the stamp.
  9. If the phone says Already stamped, this guest already has that shop. Invalid code means the code does not match the card.

Weekly drawing

Pick winners for the week

Giveaways for people who joined. Everyone who joined gets 1 entry, plus 1 more for each shop stamped.

  1. On the Organizer tab, choose the crawl first. The drawing uses only that crawl.
  2. Open Weekly drawing. The pool is everyone who joined, at every shop. The entry total sits next to the pool size.
  3. A second stamp at the same shop does not add another entry. A paper stamp counts the same as a phone stamp. Stamps on a different crawl do not count.
  4. Choose how many winners. One is the usual week. Add a label such as Week 1 if you want the history grouped.
  5. Tap Draw. The names are picked in the database, with more entries making a win more likely. The page does not choose them. One person cannot be drawn twice in the same week.
  6. A person can win once on this crawl. They can still join and win a different crawl.
  7. Each winner shows a name, email, and entry count so you can contact them. Copy winners or Download CSV keeps that list for the crawl you have open.
  8. The owner, an organizer, or a co-organizer can draw. A contributor cannot.

Guest leaderboard

See how the cards are filling

Ranks for the crawl you have selected. Stamps from every shop count.

  1. On the Organizer tab, choose the crawl first. The board uses only that crawl.
  2. Open How the cards are filling. Rank is by different shops stamped. A tie goes to the guest who reached that number first.
  3. The total is the shops on the passport: paid, approved, and visible. An unpaid shop is not part of the card.
  4. A star means the card is full. The time is Pacific time, from the stamp that finished it.
  5. You see the full name and email so you can reach a guest. Download CSV saves the crawl you have open.
  6. The list is 50 guests per page. Prev and Next move through the crawl. Page 2 continues the rank from page 1. Download CSV includes every guest, not only the page you are looking at.
  7. The owner, an organizer, or a co-organizer can open this. A contributor cannot. The store page shows a shorter name and no email.
