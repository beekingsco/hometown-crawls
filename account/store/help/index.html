Host guide

Host checklist

A short list for the counter on the Downtown Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl. Run through this before launch Saturday, Oct 31. You can read this before you sign in.

Open the Store tab Organizer checklist Support

Step 1

Sign in

  1. Open /account on the phone you will keep at the counter.
  2. Enter your name, email, and phone. Check the two consent boxes.
  3. Tap Send magic link. Open that email on this same phone. There is no password.
  4. When you land back on the account page, tap Store. A page labeled DEMO DATA is only a sample. It does not stamp a real guest.

Step 2

Find your code and QR

  1. On the Store tab, the counter code sits next to your shop name, and again under Counter code.
  2. Tap Print counter sign. Print that page. It shows the QR and the code guests type if the camera fails.
  3. Use the code on the screen today. An old photo of the sign will not work after you tap New code.
  4. A folded tent card is at the counter card. Sign in there with the shop email if you want that version.

Step 3

Stamp a guest at the counter

  1. The guest signs in with their own email magic link, on the holiday trail or on /account.
  2. They stand inside the shop, within 50 meters of the counter.
  3. They scan the QR, or they pick your shop and type the counter code.
  4. The phone asks for location. They need to allow it. The stamp lands only when they are at the shop. There is no location bypass.
  5. Their stamp fills in with color.

Location required means the phone blocked location. Allow it and try again at the counter. You need to be at the shop means they are too far. Already stamped means this guest already has your shop. That is fine.

Step 4

Optional coupon

  1. On the Store tab, open Post-stamp coupon.
  2. Check Turn on the post-stamp reward. It stays off until you do.
  3. Write the reward, set how many days it lasts, and tap Save reward.
  4. The guest sees it only after your stamp lands.

Step 5

Your shop profile

  1. On the Store tab, open Public listing.
  2. Fill the shop name, address, zip code, and hours.
  3. Add a cover photo, and a stamp image if you have one. PNG, JPG, or WebP.
  4. Tap Save listing.
  5. An organizer can finish this for you, including a shop that is not theirs. The organizer checklist has that step.

Step 6

When a guest uses a paper card

  1. Give them an ink stamp on the card. Do not type a digital code for them.
  2. They can print a card at /card.
  3. They turn the finished card in at a co-organizer shop, such as Holiday Cafe or ANTHEM.
  4. An organizer searches the guest and checks off the shops. That check is only for the paper card. It does not skip the 50 meter rule on a phone stamp.
  5. Completed paper cards must be turned in by end of day December 31, 2026 (Pacific).

Paper stamps count the same as stamps in the web app. Guests can switch to the web app anytime.

If you get stuck

Send a note from Support and include your shop name. Organizers can follow the organizer checklist, including the counter QR try at Holiday Cafe and ANTHEM.
