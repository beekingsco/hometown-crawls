FAQs

Quick answers

For crawlers collecting stamps, shops claiming seats, and organizers launching a hometown trail.

Do I need to download an app?

No. Hometown Crawls works in your mobile browser. Join a digital stamp passport, visit shops, and stamp with a QR or counter code. Save the site to your home screen if you like — still no App Store download.

How do I get a stamp?

Join the crawl passport, visit a participating shop in person, then scan the shop QR or type the printed counter code. Location may be recorded as optional soft context; we do not hard-block distant claims.

What if the QR won’t scan?

Ask for the printed code on the tent card and enter it manually on the trail page or Account → Stamp.

Is it free for crawlers?

Yes. Joining a passport is free. You support shops by visiting (and hopefully buying something delicious). Completer prizes are while supplies last each season.

Do I have to buy something at every stop?

Stamps require an in-person visit and the official QR or counter code. Buying something is the spirit of the crawl — it keeps independent shops glad to host you — but the platform does not force a purchase to claim a stamp.

How long do I have to finish a passport?

Each trail has its own season. Check the trail page for Live, Upcoming, or Ended. Finish within the season if you want that season’s completer prize.

How do shop seats work?

Shops pay a monthly seat (e.g. $49/mo for Puyallup Coffee, $59/mo for Puyallup Pub). You get a map pin, passport slot, QR, and counter code. Organizers and Hometown Crawls split seat revenue 50/50.

What does a shop receive with a seat?

A listed stop on the trail, a unique QR for crawlers to scan, a printed-ready counter code for when cameras fail, and visitors who are already looking for independent stops in town.

Can I organize a crawl in my town?

Absolutely — organizing is free. Start on the organize page. You invite shops, they claim seats, and you keep half of every seat.

Who can organize — chambers, downtown groups, volunteers?

Chambers of commerce, downtown associations, neighborhood groups, and individual volunteers are all welcome. See our organizer guide for a calm overview.

Do you use Google / Apple sign-in?

No SSO buttons. Sign in with an email magic link, or continue anonymously where available.

Are pub crawls 21+ only?

Yes. Pub crawls are 21+ with valid ID where alcohol is served. Drink responsibly; stamps do not require a purchase of alcohol.
