Guide · Organizers

For chamber & hometown organizers

Launch a coffee or pub trail without building an app. Shops claim monthly seats; you keep half of every seat. Hometown Crawls runs the digital passport.

Chambers, downtown associations, and community volunteers already know which independent shops make a town feel like itself. Hometown Crawls turns that knowledge into a walkable passport: crawlers stamp in the browser, shops get a clear seat on the map, and organizers earn a simple revenue share.

What you get

  • Free to organize — no platform fee to apply or launch.
  • 50/50 on shop seats — coffee seats are typically $49/mo; pub seats $59/mo. You keep half; Hometown Crawls keeps half for the product.
  • Passports without an app — easier asks for residents and visitors alike.
  • QR + counter codes — shops can stamp even when cameras struggle.

A calm onboarding path

Apply on the organize page with your town, trail type (coffee or pub), and contact details. After review, you recruit independent stops. Each shop claims a seat (see join as a shop), receives QR materials, and appears on the trail map and passport.

Who this fits

Main-street programs, coffee crawls tied to a festival weekend, year-round “support local” challenges, and 21+ pub trails where venues want foot traffic without another proprietary app. If you already meet monthly with merchants, you already have the hardest part: relationships.

Tips that keep shops happy

  • Recruit a coherent set of stops — walkable clusters beat a sprawling list.
  • Set expectations: stamps are in person; codes stay in the shop.
  • Share the crawler guide and FAQs so staff hear the same story crawlers do.
  • Announce completer prizes only when inventory and season dates are real — never invent awards.
