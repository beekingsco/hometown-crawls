Prizes

Finish the trail, claim a prize

Complete your digital stamp passport and unlock seasonal completer rewards — a thank-you for walking your hometown and supporting independent stops.

Hometown Crawls completer prizes are for crawlers who finish a trail the honest way: in-person stamps at every participating shop on your passport. Exact prize items change by season and trail; details appear on the trail page and at partner shops when a season is live.

We do not invent or list fake awards here. When a season’s prize is announced, you will see it on the trail and in shop windows — not as padded marketing copy.

How claiming works

When your passport shows every stop stamped, follow the claim instructions on the trail page for that season. Hometown Crawls may confirm stamp history before fulfillment. If supplies run out, we will say so clearly — better an honest “sold out” than a hollow promise.

Shop shout-outs

Some stops may offer their own punch-card specials or seasonal treats on top of the crawl prize. Those are set by each shop; ask at the counter. They do not replace the trail completer prize, and they are not Hometown Crawls awards.

Fair play

Shared codes, remote stamps, or multiple accounts to farm prizes void eligibility. Read the full rules before you race to the finish.
