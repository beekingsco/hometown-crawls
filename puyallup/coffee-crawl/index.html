Questions

When is the crawl?

The Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl starts November 1, 2026 and runs through December 31. Crawlers can visit stops any time during your normal business hours.

What is the $49.99 for?

It helps cover printed passports, marketing materials, and website costs for the crawl.

Is the $49.99 a subscription?

No. It's a one-time fee per location for this crawl. You won't be charged again.

What do I need to do at my shop?

Display the QR sign we send you near the register, and let your team know the counter code. Guests scan or type the code to collect their stamp. You don't need to change your POS or add any hardware.

Do guests have to buy something?

That's up to you. Most shops ask for a purchase to collect the stamp, and that's the norm for this crawl.

What happens after I pay?

You'll get a Stripe receipt right away. Before the crawl starts, we'll email your QR sign, counter code, and a preview of your map listing.

Who do I contact with questions?

Email bryan@myanthemcoffee.com, or reply to your Stripe receipt.
