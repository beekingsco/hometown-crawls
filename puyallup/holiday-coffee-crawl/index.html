Questions

Is it free for players?

Yes. Getting a passport and collecting stamps costs nothing. Order what you like when you visit a shop.

How do stamps work?

Join the passport, go to a participating shop, and scan that shop's QR code or type the counter code. Each shop stamps once.

When are shops announced?

Shops show up on this page after they join and their listing is confirmed. Until then you'll see "Shops announced soon."

What if I started on a paper card?

Paper stamps count the same as stamps in the web app. You can switch to the web app anytime. Bring a finished paper card to a co-organizer shop by the end of December 31, 2026.

What are the prizes?

We've locked in prize funding for the Downtown Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl. Expect weekly giveaways through the crawl (including prizes that spotlight other downtown businesses), a special participation prize for the first 50 guests who complete the whole passport (we're looking at something like a custom coffee travel mug), and grand prizes we'll announce as soon as those details are set. Fill your passport to be in the running. Official Rules apply.
