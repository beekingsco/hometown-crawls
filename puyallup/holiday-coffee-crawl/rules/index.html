Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl: Official Rules

These rules cover the Puyallup Holiday Coffee Crawl from November 1 through December 31, 2026. This crawl does not include a drawing or awards.

How to take part

  • Visit a participating shop in person.
  • One stamp per shop.
  • Scan the shop QR code or enter its counter code while you are at the shop.
  • Paper stamps count the same as stamps in the web app. You can switch to the web app anytime.
  • Show some love if you can and buy something at the shop. A purchase is not required to get a stamp.

Paper cards

Completed paper cards must be turned in by end of day December 31, 2026 (Pacific). Bring a finished card to a co-organizer shop. An ink stamp on paper counts the same as a stamp in the web app.

Fair play

  • Do not share QR codes or counter codes outside the shop.
  • You must be at the shop when you stamp.
  • Be kind to the people working the counter.

Privacy

Sign-in and stamp details are handled as described in the Hometown Crawls Privacy Policy.

Organizer

The crawl is organized by Miracle Studios, Inc., 204 Still Glen Drive, Terrell, Texas 75160.
