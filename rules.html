Rules

Play fair, stamp honest

Simple guidelines so every coffee crawl stays fun for crawlers, shops, and neighbors on Main Street.

Hometown Crawls is a digital stamp passport for real sidewalks. The rules below protect independent shops and leave room for kindness at the counter.

Visiting shops

Every stamp starts with showing up. Remote stamping, photo-sharing of codes, or sending a friend to “grab you a stamp” breaks the trust shops place in the trail.

  • Stamps must be claimed in person at the participating shop.
  • Scan the official QR or enter the printed counter code on the tent card.
  • Be kind to staff — they are hosting your adventure between real customers.
  • Show some love if you can and buy something at the shop.
  • Respect posted hours, seating limits, and neighborhood quiet.

Passports & stamps

Your passport is personal. Progress should reflect your own walks through town.

  • One stamp per shop per passport (unless a trail page says otherwise).
  • Sharing QR secrets or counter codes online, in group chats, or on social media voids your progress.
  • Paper stamps count the same as stamps in the web app. You can switch to the web app anytime.
  • You must be at the shop. Location is required, and a claim farther than 50 meters from a shop that has a map pin is refused. A shop without a pin can still be stamped once location is shared.
  • Join the correct passport for the trail you are walking.

Shops & organizers

  • Display your QR and tent card where staff can help crawlers quickly.
  • Do not share seat codes outside your location; seats are for your storefront.
  • Organizers should recruit real, independent stops and keep trail pages accurate.

Conduct

Harassment of staff or other crawlers, vandalism, or unsafe behavior can result in passport removal. When in doubt, leave the shop better than you found it.
